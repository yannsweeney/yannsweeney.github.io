# AI Use Disclosure

I use approved AI tools to assist with administrative tasks, reviewer search, enhanced search, literature discovery and understanding, tool development, and workflow management. 

When assessing a manuscript I often use an AI chat alongside the manuscript files and use it to surface relevant information from these files and find relevant papers in the literature.

When assessing revisions or appeals I often use AI tools to summarise changes made, and the issues that were responded to or not.

I do not use AI tools to write emails (except for the occasional suggested one-liner responses) or decision letters, and rarely use them for summarising those that I receive.

AI tools do not make or advise on editorial decisions. Narrow technical assessments and reviewer suggestions are reviewed and approved by me.

Confidential manuscript information is handled in accordance with Springer Nature policies and applicable confidentiality requirements. Currently this primarily involves interacting with Microsoft 365 Copilot.
